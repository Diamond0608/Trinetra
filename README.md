## What is Trinetra?

Trinetra is a wearable-tech CAD and mechanism-design project. It is a compact wrist-worn enclosure with a spring-loaded latching mechanism that extends and retracts an arm at the press of a button. It is meant as a utility tool.

I designed it in Fusion 360 to practise fitting moving parts into a tight space. Most of the work was re-placing components and redoing joints until nothing collided.

**Status:** complete in CAD, not built.
